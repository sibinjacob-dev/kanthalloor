Popular Destinations

Discover the most beloved tourist spots in Kerala, featuring lush landscapes and vibrant biodiversity.

Kanthalloor

Explore Kerala's winter fruit basket, famous for its rare apple orchards, strawberry farms, and terraced vegetable cultivation in a cool, misty climate.

Marayoor

Discover the only place in Kerala with natural sandalwood forests, ancient pre-historic dolmens (Muniyaras), and the region's famous sweet jaggery

Munnar Waterfalls

Take a dip in the pristine, cascading waters of Lakkam, Attukal, and Thoovanam falls, surrounded by lush green forests and tea estates

Forest Trecking
